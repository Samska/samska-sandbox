## SS-034 — Product Media Upload (#57)

- Completed and merged via PR #58 (squash commit `31dcdef`, merged 2026-09-25): one validated, re-encoded JPEG per Product stored with the Product in bounded backend process memory under one Catalog store lock, with fixed limits (3 MiB multipart request, 2 MiB input file, 2048 pixels per side, 4 million pixels total, 2 MiB normalized JPEG output, 32 MiB aggregate stored bytes); exhausted capacity returns `413` with the distinct `storage-capacity-exceeded` code, and rejection changes nothing. Products carry a read-only nullable `uploadedMediaId`; uploaded media takes display precedence on Market cards and Product detail with the curated `mediaKey` asset and the monogram fallback retained; full `PUT` preserves uploaded media. The Admin surface is route-based: `/admin/products` is management-only, `/admin/products/new` creates first with `mediaKey: null` and then uploads with retry and no duplicate create, and `/admin/products/{id}/edit` stages replacement or confirmed removal, reports field/media partial success for the separate requests, and reconciles uncertain outcomes by reading the Product. [ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-adopt-product-media-upload.md) supersedes the no-upload decision of [ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-adopt-optional-product-media-reference.md); the accepted EXIF orientation limitation and local-only aggregate-cap risk remain.
- Verification: Agent Verification passed (backend 99 tests; frontend typecheck, 107 tests across 12 files, production build); Human Verification owner-reported approved 2026-09-25 including the targeted create/edit image recheck; CI passed on the exact final head `7c793c95452ade8cd57c1a50ffe05f06c40077c6` before the squash merge. ## SS-033 — Admin Catalog Management (#55)

- Completed and merged via PR #56 (squash commit `5ac10a4`): a distinct `/admin/products` surface lists Products with ID, name, description, price, and media, filters locally by case-insensitive name, and creates, edits, and deletes Products through `POST`, full-replacement `PUT /api/products/{id}`, and `DELETE /api/products/{id}` after explicit inline confirmation; absent IDs return `404`, and deletion returns `409 Conflict` while the Product is in the current Cart.
