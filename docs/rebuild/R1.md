## Final review round and process note

After the visual pass, two small changes were made by the Orquestrador itself (`block: "start"` for the detail reveal; two-line reservation for card names so amounts align). The gap was closed with two independent read-only reviews of the frozen state:

- **Design UX/UI — approved, no blockers.** Change (A) passes and behaves consistently with a short grid (heading lands at viewport top; 623-857px of room below) and a long grid; the tablet case lands at `top` 127 only because the document reaches maximum scroll, still fully visible. Two cosmetic lows remain (tight brand strip below 768px; a lone card when the count is `4n+1`).
