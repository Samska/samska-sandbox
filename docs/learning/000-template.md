# SS-000: Learning Record Title

- Status: Draft, Active, Superseded, or Historical
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Work item: [SS-000](https://github.com/Samska/samska-sandbox/issues/000)
- Pull request: [PR #000](https://github.com/Samska/samska-sandbox/pull/000)
- ADRs: None or links
- Canonical documentation: Links

## Goal and Previous State

State the problem, requested outcome, and the relevant starting state. Identify which claims are historical facts and which describe the current state.

## What Changed and Why

Summarize the material change and the evidence-supported reason for it. Link to canonical sources instead of duplicating their full content.

## Engineering Concepts

For each central concept, explain:

- What the concept means in this work.
- How Samska applies it.
- Why that application was selected.
- A relevant trade-off, constraint, or failure mode.

## Alternatives and Trade-offs

List only alternatives that were actually considered or are explicitly documented. Describe what was accepted and what remains deferred.

## Security and Verification

Describe material security implications, verification performed, and meaningful verification gaps. Do not claim a control or test ran without evidence.

## Failure Modes and Safeguards

Describe realistic ways the decision or implementation could fail, drift, or be misused, together with the applicable safeguard or follow-up.

## Plan, Build, Review, and Pull Request Lessons

### Plan

Record the durable constraints, questions, and selected direction. Do not reproduce prompts or conversations.

### Build

Record meaningful implementation facts and deviations from the plan.

### Review

Record evidence-backed findings, decisions, and verification gaps. If review evidence is absent, say so rather than inferring it.

### Pull Request

Record the scope, evidence, deferred work, and residual risks documented by the pull request.

## What the Project Owner Should Understand

Summarize the few concepts and decisions the owner should be able to explain accurately.

## Interview Practice

### Questions

- Question based on this work?

### Interview-Ready Explanation

Provide a concise explanation based on what Samska actually did, including a trade-off and an evidence boundary.

## Follow-up and Sources

List future work, supersession notes, and links to the Issue, Pull Request, ADRs, canonical documentation, and any other evidence.
